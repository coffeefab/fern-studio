How we handle your information

Privacy Policy

Last updated · May 19, 2026

Fern Event Rentals ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have about it.

Information We Collect

Booking and contact information

When you submit a booking request, place an order, or contact us, we collect the information you provide: name, email address, phone number, mailing and event address, event date and times, and any notes or special requests.

Payment information

We do not store credit card numbers or banking details on our website. Card payments are processed by Stripe through Booqable. Stripe receives your card details under its own privacy practices; we receive a record of the transaction (date, amount, last 4 digits of the card) but not the full card number.

Browsing information

Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) and embedded third-party services may log basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, for security and operational purposes.

Local storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember items you add to your cart between visits. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to booking inquiries and prepare quotes
  • To deliver, pick up, and service your rental order
  • To process payments (deposit and final balance)
  • To send order confirmations, reminders, and pickup or delivery details
  • To comply with tax, accounting, and other legal recordkeeping obligations
  • To improve our service quality and customer experience

Who We Share It With

We share information only with the third parties we rely on to operate the business:

  • Booqable for order management, inventory, and the rental agreement at checkout
  • Stripe for credit and debit card processing
  • Web3Forms for routing booking-form submissions to our email inbox
  • Google (Gmail) for email correspondence with you

We do not sell your personal information to anyone. We do not share it for advertising or marketing purposes outside of the providers listed above.

Marketing Communications

If we send you promotional emails or text messages, you can opt out at any time by replying STOP to a text message or using the unsubscribe link in an email. You will continue to receive transactional messages related to bookings you have placed.

Data Retention

We keep booking and order records for as long as needed to provide services and to meet our legal, tax, and accounting obligations (typically 7 years from the date of the event). After that, records are deleted or anonymized.

Your Rights

You may request:

  • A copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction of information that is inaccurate or out of date
  • Deletion of your information, subject to our legal recordkeeping obligations

Send requests to byfernstudio@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we collect, including limiting access to authorized personnel and relying on reputable third-party providers (Stripe, Booqable, Web3Forms, Google) for the systems that store or process customer data. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the current version. Material changes will be communicated by email when feasible.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Reach us at byfernstudio@gmail.com or (317) 442-9621.
