About us

From cheesecloth runners
to event essentials.

Four years ago we launched ByFernStudio, an online shop specializing in cheesecloth table runners and napkins, dyed by hand in small batches. That journey pulled us deep into the world of events, connecting us with hosts and couples from around the globe as they brought their celebrations to life.

Today we're expanding with the essentials for any event: tables, chairs, tents, flower walls, arches, drapery, LED signs, and more. Our promise stays the same: quality pieces at fair prices, delivered and styled with care.

When you reach out, you're talking to us directly. We deliver every rental and personally install flower walls and statement pieces. Breakdown is available for an additional fee. No surprises, no inflated quotes, just two people who care about how your event turns out.
