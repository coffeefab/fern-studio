Terms & Conditions

Last updated · May 19, 2026

This Rental Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between Fern Studio Event Rentals ("Company") and the Renter. By submitting payment or signing this Agreement, the Renter agrees to the terms and conditions outlined below.

Agreement of terms

By submitting the required 25% nonrefundable deposit or signing this Agreement, the Renter acknowledges that they have read, understood, and agreed to all terms and conditions herein. This Agreement constitutes a binding contract between the Renter and Fern Studio Event Rentals.

Confirming your rental

Order accuracy

The Renter is responsible for verifying that all rental items, quantities, event dates, and times are correct. Same day changes to items or rental times cannot be accommodated.

Order adjustments

Any changes must be requested at least 72 hours prior to the event date and are subject to item availability and approval.

Right of refusal

Fern Studio Event Rentals reserves the right to refuse service to any individual or event at its discretion.

Payment

Deposit

A 25% nonrefundable deposit is due at the time of booking. The event date is not secured until this payment is received.

Final balance

The remaining balance must be paid in full prior to delivery or pickup of rental items. Failure to do so may result in cancellation without refund.

Delivery

Location & access

Delivery will be made to the address provided by the Renter. The Renter must ensure a clear, safe, and accessible path to the dropoff location (e.g., driveway, backyard, garage, or indoor space).

Delivery window

While we strive to arrive within the agreed time window, delays may occur due to traffic, weather, or other unforeseen circumstances. Such delays do not constitute a breach of contract.

Customer pickup

Scheduling

Renter arranged pickups must occur at the scheduled time. The Renter must arrive within a 15 minute window before or after the scheduled pickup time.

Failure to pick up

If the Renter fails to arrive within the 15 minute pickup window, the rental may be considered forfeited at Fern Studio Event Rentals' discretion. In such cases, the Renter will forfeit the 25% nonrefundable deposit as well as the full remaining balance, and no refunds will be issued.

Third party pickup

If a third party is picking up the rentals, the Renter must provide the individual's name and phone number in advance. The 15 minute pickup window still applies.

Pickup readiness & teardown

Item preparation

For rentals scheduled for pickup by Fern Studio Event Rentals, all items must be folded, stacked, and ready at the agreed time.

Teardown fee

Items not prepared for pickup may result in a teardown fee or an additional full day rental charge.

Large installations

Only select items such as flower walls and other large installations will be assembled and disassembled by Fern Studio Event Rentals. Once assembled, an installation cannot be moved from its setup location. Moving will result in an additional fine.

Care of equipment

Proper use

All rental items must be used only for their intended purpose and handled with care.

Weather protection

The Renter is responsible for protecting all items from weather exposure, including rain, wind, snow, and excessive moisture.

Damage & loss

The Renter assumes full financial responsibility for any lost, stolen, damaged, or excessively soiled items, including but not limited to stains, burns, breakage, or missing parts.

Outdoor events

For outdoor events, the Renter must have a weather contingency plan. Fern Studio Event Rentals reserves the right to modify delivery or pickup schedules to protect staff and equipment.

Use of images & media

Fern Studio Event Rentals reserves the right to photograph or record rented items before, during, or after the event and to use such images or videos for marketing, promotional, website, and social media purposes. This includes images of installations, décor, and event setups. If the Renter wishes to opt out of this use, written notice must be provided prior to the event date.

Cancellations

More than 7 days prior

Cancellations made more than 7 days before the event will result in forfeiture of the 25% nonrefundable deposit.

Within 7 days

Cancellations made within 7 days of the event will result in forfeiture of 100% of the rental fee.

Severe weather

In cases of severe or unsafe weather, Fern Studio Event Rentals will work with the Renter to explore rescheduling or partial refunds at our discretion.

Force majeure

Fern Studio Event Rentals shall not be held liable for failure or delay in performance due to events beyond our reasonable control, including but not limited to acts of God, extreme weather, natural disasters, fire, flood, government restrictions, power outages, labor disruptions, or other emergencies. In such cases, Fern Studio Event Rentals will make reasonable efforts to reschedule services or provide a partial refund at our discretion, but is not obligated to do so.
